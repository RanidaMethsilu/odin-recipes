Fried Rice Recipe

Sri Lankan-style chicken and vegetable fried rice features aromatic spices like curry and pandan leaves, lots of leeks and carrots, and smoky, high-heat wok frying.


Ingredients

  • Rice: 3 to 4 cups day-old cooked Basmati, Keeri Samba, or Samba rice.
  • Protein: 250g to 400g boneless chicken (boiled, shredded, and lightly fried) and 2 to 3 eggs.
  • Aromatics: 1 sliced onion, 4 minced garlic cloves, 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste, 3 sprigs of curry leaves, and a 3-inch piece of pandan leaf (rampe).
  • Vegetables: 1 to 2 julienned or grated carrots, 1 to 2 leeks (separated into white and green parts), and optional green beans or cabbage.
  • Seasoning & Sauces: 1 to 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp chili flakes (or Sri Lankan chili paste), 1/2 tsp black pepper, salt to taste, and 1 tbsp butter or oil for flavor.

Steps

  1. Prepare the Rice: Cook your rice ahead of time so it is cool and firm (day-old chilled rice works best to prevent mushiness).
  2. Cook the Chicken: Boil the chicken breasts with a pinch of salt until tender, shred them, and pan-fry them lightly in oil until golden. Set aside.
  3. Scramble the Eggs: Heat a little oil or butter in a wide wok or heavy pan. Crack the eggs in, scramble them into nice chunks, and remove them.
  4. Sauté the Aromatics: Add a fresh tablespoon of oil and butter to the same pan. Sauté the chopped onions, minced garlic, ginger-garlic paste, curry leaves, and pandan leaf until fragrant.
  5. Add the Vegetables: Toss in the carrots and the white/light green parts of the leeks first, cooking them for 1–2 minutes on medium-high heat.
  6. Combine Rice and Seasonings: Add the cold cooked rice, shredded chicken, scrambled eggs, chili flakes, black pepper, and soy sauce.
  7. High-Heat Toss: Turn the heat up high and stir-fry everything vigorously for 2 to 3 minutes.
  8. Finish: Stir in the dark green leek tops right at the end for a fresh crunch. Serve hot alongside chili paste or a chicken curry.
