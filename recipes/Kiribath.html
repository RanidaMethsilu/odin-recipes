Kiribath Recipe

Sri Lankan Kiribath (milk rice) is a rich, creamy dish made by cooking white rice with thick coconut milk, traditionally served sliced into diamonds or squares for celebrations and special occasions.


Ingredients

  • 2 cups white short-grain rice (traditionally Sri Lankan Kekulu rice)
  • 3 ¾ to 4 cups water
  • 400 ml (about 1⅔ cups / 1 can) thick coconut milk
  • 2 tsp salt (adjust to taste)
  • 1–2 inch pandan leaf (rampe) — optional, for aroma

Steps

  1. Wash the Rice: Rinse the rice in cold water 2 to 3 times until the water runs clear, then drain.
  2. Cook the Rice: Place the rinsed rice into a pot or rice cooker with the water and pandan leaf (if using). Cook until the rice is very tender, soft, and nearly all the water is absorbed.
  3. Add Coconut Milk and Salt: Pour the thick coconut milk (mixed with the salt) directly into the hot, soft rice.
  4. Simmer and Thicken: Stir well and cook on low heat for about 5 to 10 minutes until the rice absorbs the coconut milk, becomes thick and creamy, and begins to pull away from the sides of the pan.
  5. Shape and Set: Transfer the hot mixture onto a clean banana leaf, a tray, or a flat plate. Use a spatula or the back of a flat spoon (or a piece of plastic wrap/banana leaf) to press and flatten it into an even slab about 2 cm thick.
  6. Slice and Serve: Allow it to cool and firm up for 10 to 15 minutes. Grease or coat a knife with a little coconut milk or water and slice the kiribath into diamond-shaped pieces or squares.
