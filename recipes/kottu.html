Kottu Recipe

Sri Lankan Chicken Kottu Roti is a popular street food made from chopped parata roti, vegetables, eggs, and spicy chicken curry.


Ingredients

  • 4 to 6 Godamba rotis (or frozen parathas/roti canai), cooked and sliced into strips
  • 2 to 4 eggs
  • 1 cup shredded or diced chicken from a pre-cooked chicken curry (plus 1 cup of spicy chicken curry gravy)
  • 1 red onion - sliced
  • 1 cup white cabbage - shredded
  • 1/2 to 1 cup carrots - julienned or cut into matchsticks
  • 1 leek, sliced (white and green parts)
  • 1 to 2 green chilies or banana peppers- sliced
  • 10 fresh curry leaves
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste (or freshly minced ginger and garlic)
  • 1 to 2 tsp Sri Lankan roasted curry powder
  • 1 tsp chili flakes or chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 to 3 tbsp oil (coconut or sunflower oil)
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Prep the Roti: Cook the Godamba rotis on a hot griddle if fresh, then stack them, roll them up, and chop them into small ribbons or bite-sized chunks.
  2. Sauté Aromatics: Heat oil in a large wok or flat iron pan over medium-high heat. Add the curry leaves, sliced onions, green chilies, and ginger-garlic paste. Sauté for 1–2 minutes until fragrant.
  3. Cook the Vegetables: Toss in the shredded cabbage, carrots, and leeks. Stir-fry briskly for 2 to 3 minutes until slightly tender yet crisp.
  4. Scramble the Eggs: Push the vegetables to the side of the pan. Crack the eggs directly into the empty space, season with a pinch of salt, and scramble until cooked through, then mix them into the vegetables.
  5. Add Chicken and Spices: Add the shredded chicken, Sri Lankan curry powder, chili flakes, black pepper, and soy sauce. Toss everything together.
  6. Incorporate Roti & Gravy: Add the chopped roti pieces and pour in the spicy chicken curry gravy.
  7. Toss and Beat: Use two spatulas (or metal scrapers) to rhythmically chop, toss, and combine all the ingredients over high heat for about 1 to 2 minutes so the roti absorbs the savory sauce without getting soggy.
  8. Serve: Serve hot immediately, paired with extra chicken curry or chili paste on the side
